    How to compress a JPG image

    1. Upload your JPG image, or drag it onto the page.
    2. Choose a compression level — Normal, Strong, or Extreme.
    3. Preview the compressed result and compare the file sizes.
    4. Download the compressed JPG image.

    Why compress JPG images?

    JPG is the most common photo format on the web, but photos straight from a camera or phone are often far larger than they need to be. Compressing a JPG reduces its file size, which speeds up page loading, saves bandwidth, and makes images easier to share by email or chat.

    Your images are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to a server.

    JPG compression FAQ

    Does compressing JPG reduce quality?

    Yes — JPG compression is lossy, so lower quality settings produce smaller files with a slight reduction in detail. The "Normal" level keeps quality high while still shrinking the file.

    How much can a JPG be compressed?

    It depends on the image. Photos often compress by 50–80% with little visible difference. Start with Normal (75) and adjust if you need a smaller file.

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. All processing happens locally in your browser.
