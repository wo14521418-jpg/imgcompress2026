    How to compress an image

    1. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
    2. Choose a compression level — Normal, Strong, or Extreme.
    3. Preview the result and compare the original and compressed sizes.
    4. Download the compressed image.

    Why use this image compressor?

    Images are often the largest part of a web page. Compressing them before you upload makes pages load faster, saves bandwidth, and improves your site's performance. This tool works entirely in your browser — your images never leave your device.

    Supported formats

    • JPG — the standard format for photos.
    • PNG — lossless, with support for transparency.
    • WebP — a modern format that is smaller than JPG and PNG.
