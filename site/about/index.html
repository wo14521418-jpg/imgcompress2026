About Us

Our mission is simple: to make image compression fast, private, and accessible to everyone — so the web stays quick and lightweight for people everywhere.

Every day, millions of images are uploaded to the internet, and far too many of them are larger than they need to be. Oversized images slow websites down, frustrate visitors, and drag search rankings lower. Image Compressor exists to solve that problem in the simplest possible way: open the page, drop in an image, and download an optimized version within seconds. No sign-up, no uploads, no waiting.

We are an independent project, built and maintained with care by a solo developer rather than a faceless company. That means a few things worth knowing: we treat your privacy as non-negotiable — every image is processed entirely in your browser and never touches a server; we keep the tool genuinely free and free of bloat; and we actually read your feedback, so feature requests and bug reports shape what gets built next.

Whether you are a developer shipping a faster website, a designer exporting assets, a content creator preparing uploads, or simply someone who needs a smaller file, this tool was built for you. Thank you for using it.
