    How to compress multiple images at once

    1. Select several images (use Ctrl/Cmd to multi-select, or drag them in).
    2. Choose a compression level — it applies to every image.
    3. Watch the progress as each image is compressed.
    4. Download all compressed images as a single ZIP file.

    Why batch compress images?

    If you have a folder of photos or a set of product images, compressing them one by one is slow. Batch compression lets you process them all at once with a single quality setting, then download everything as one ZIP archive. It all happens locally in your browser.

    Batch compression FAQ

    How many images can I compress at once?

    There is no hard limit. A very large batch may take longer depending on your device's performance.

    Do I need to download each image separately?

    No — all compressed images are bundled into a single ZIP file.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Everything is processed locally in your browser.
