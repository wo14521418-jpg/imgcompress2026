    Why compress WebP images?

    WebP is already an efficient format, but a WebP exported at a high quality setting can still be larger than necessary. Re-compressing a WebP at a lower quality level can shrink it further — useful when you need to squeeze every kilobyte for a fast website. Processing happens locally in your browser.

    WebP compression FAQ

    What is WebP?

    WebP is a modern image format from Google that offers better compression than JPG and PNG, with support for transparency and animation.

    Does compressing WebP reduce quality?

    Yes — lowering the quality setting produces a smaller file with slightly less detail. Start with Normal (75).

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. All processing happens locally in your browser.
